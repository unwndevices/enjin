## Executive Summary

v1.5 ships 30/34 requirements satisfied. The blocking issue is **RENDER-01 (Phase 27 never started)**. Three additional partial gaps exist: the `engine.scene.switch()` and `engine.scene.find()` live wiring paths are broken by a registration-order issue, and `init(self)` does not receive the proxy when a script is loaded from file.

Phases 28–37 are functionally complete with 18 ctests passing. The 11 phases delivered a substantial scripting foundation: float dt, named objects, scene self-transitions, the engine.* Lua table, ScriptProxy userdata, ScriptErrorPolicy, input event callbacks, GC control, component assertions, drawable cache decoupling, and codebase hardening.
